How the generator is scoring

The current automatic model starts with the market's vig-adjusted probability, then adjusts for the difference between the two probable starters' season ERAs. It caps the pitching adjustment so one statistic cannot dominate. It then searches for a price/model gap. This is intentionally more conservative than pretending we have lineup and bullpen information when we do not. Once confirmed lineups are available, those should be checked before placing a bet.
